# Event formats at a glance

Status: summary of supplied guidance imported 2026-10-20; no live policy verification.

| Item | Workshops | Studio Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frequency | 4 per term | 8 over roughly half an academic year |
| Length | 60 minutes | 60 minutes |
| Attendance | 25+ students | 10+ students |
| Purpose | Guided hands-on activity; students build and compare work | Students bring projects and questions, work together, and get unstuck |
| Preparation | Copy the master deck; plan an audience-appropriate activity with co-Lead | Choose a recurring time/place; invite students with work to do |
| Calendar | University Luma event, submitted as the same existing event to central calendar | University Luma event; central workshop calendar submission not required |
| Approval | Submit at least 48 hours before start; wait for approval before promoting or hosting | Supplied Studio Session guide does not specify this workshop approval process |
| Arrival | Check-in opens 2 hours before start when approved on the Global Luma calendar | Share the Studio Session check-in form |
| Access/credits | Check-in includes claiming ChatGPT Plus | Help students access Plus if needed; give $20 Codex credits before they leave, using the program process |
| Closing | Check-out form, available up to 3 hours after end; invite to community/Studio Sessions | Help students continue building; no workshop check-out rule stated here |
| Report | Workshop Recap workflow in #collective-events | Studio Session Recap workflow in #collective-events |

These are the source's attendance figures, not claims that any event has taken place or that attendance was achieved.

Sources: [S001 workshops](../sources/originals/workshops.txt), [S002 Studio Sessions](../sources/originals/studio-sessions.md).

## Showcases

The [bundled event formats S004](../sources/marketing-kit/documents/Event-Formats.md) describe showcases as opportunities to celebrate student work with AI, usually at term end. No frequency, length, attendance requirement, or approval/check-in process was supplied. Do not assume prizes, judging, or a competition.

Choose the [workshop playbook](../playbooks/workshops.md) or [Studio Session playbook](../playbooks/studio-sessions.md) for the full operational process.
