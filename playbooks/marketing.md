# Market events and use the brand

Sources: [S003 supplied marketing guidance](../sources/originals/marketing.md), plus the [bundled marketing guide S004](../sources/marketing-kit/documents/Marketing-Guide.md) and [creative guide S004](../sources/marketing-kit/documents/Creative-Guide.md). Imported 2026-10-20; not a live verification of the brand policy.

## Message and attribution

- Clearly name **OpenAI Student Collective** as the organiser. Make the student-led relationship clear and do not imply that OpenAI directly hosts the event.
- Say who the event is for and what students will make, learn, or try. Make specific, realistic promises that fit that audience.
- Name the product actually used: Codex or ChatGPT Work, following the supplied marketing guidance. Do not invent product availability or access promises.
- Finished materials need the **date, time, and location**. Reference artwork contains sample event details, not confirmed details for a new event.
- Include the real Luma RSVP link, and for workshop posters a QR code pointing to that link. Do not invent a scannable code or event URL.

The bundled marketing guide clarifies that making the student-led relationship clear does not require literally printing “student-led” on every design. Preserve correct attribution in the final wording. Ask the Community Coordinator if wording is unclear.

## Blossom and identity

- Use the supplied Blossom as-is. Do not redraw, remix, stretch, rotate, or add effects.
- Keep it black or white, with clear space and a simple background.
- If using it, keep the full OpenAI Student Collective name clearly visible beside it. Do not pair it with the OpenAI wordmark.
- The bundled creative guide treats the Blossom as optional. Follow the official brand guidance when producing finished artwork; verify current guidance if it matters to the task.
- Supplied campus logos are assets, not evidence of permission, co-hosting, or a confirmed campus label. Ask before adding campus affiliation to a new event graphic.
