# Run a Studio Session

Source: [S002 supplied Studio Session guidance](../sources/originals/studio-sessions.md). Imported 2026-10-20; live links and policy freshness unverified.

Frequency: **8 over roughly half an academic year**. Length: **60 minutes**. Attendance: **10+ students**.

## Purpose

Give students a regular time and place to work on projects, receive peer support, and resolve questions. The format can resemble office hours or a build hour. Help people make progress on something meaningful using ChatGPT or Codex and leave with confidence to continue.

## Get ready with your co-Lead

- [ ] Choose a time and place students can return to: a library, lounge, or common area suitable for working and talking.
- [ ] Create the event on the university's Luma calendar. **Studio Sessions do not need submission to the central workshop calendar.**
- [ ] Aim for around ten students with something to work on. Invite people from workshops, classes, and clubs; encourage them to bring a friend. Share the Luma link.
- [ ] Review Slack announcements for ChatGPT and Codex updates to highlight. Access and currency must be checked at planning time.

## During the session

- [ ] Share the Studio Session check-in form/QR when students arrive and ask them to check in.
- [ ] Help students start with the needed tools. Give access to ChatGPT Plus if they do not already have it, through the program's Credits & Subscriptions process.
- [ ] Leave time for students to work. Encourage questions, connect peers who can help, and invite students to share their projects.
- [ ] Before students leave, give **$20 Codex credits** to help them keep building, using the program process. Confirm eligibility and issuance steps on the access-controlled credits page; do not invent redemption details or claim that credits were issued.

## Afterwards

- [ ] In #collective-events, select **Studio Session Recap**. This is the event report.
- [ ] Record actual progress and follow-ups in the event brief and [active work](../operations/active-work.md).

Use [resources](../knowledge/resources.md) for Luma, announcements, check-in QR, credits, and recap links. Workshop check-in/check-out timing and workshop approval deadlines are not stated as Studio Session requirements in this source.
