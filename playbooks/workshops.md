# Run a workshop

Source: [S001 supplied workshop guidance](../sources/originals/workshops.txt). Useful links are also present in the [bundled workshop checklist S004](../sources/marketing-kit/documents/Workshop-Checklist.md). Imported 2026-10-20; live links and policy freshness unverified.

Frequency: **4 per term**. Length: **60 minutes**. Attendance: **25+ students**.

## Prepare with your co-Lead

- [ ] Copy the workshop master deck and preserve its structure, including check-in and check-out slides. Work from one shared deck with your co-Lead.
- [ ] Choose an audience through a club, class, community, or campus network. If a potential co-host is connected to an AI company, check with the program team first.
- [ ] Choose a hands-on activity that fits the audience's interests and experience. The supplied Activity Library has ten beginner-friendly activities for a phone or computer using ChatGPT Work; activities can be adapted or created.
- [ ] Give the event a concrete, appealing title describing what people will make, learn, or try.
- [ ] Set a date/time and book a room with a screen and space for students to work. Follow university requirements.
- [ ] Create one event in the university's Luma calendar. Check that calendar's name, artwork, and settings first.
- [ ] Include an appropriate event image, location, date, accurate start/end times, and activity description. Keep RSVPs open to anyone. Use “OpenAI Student Collective” where naming the organiser; do not imply direct OpenAI hosting.
- [ ] Submit **at least 48 hours before the workshop starts**. On the central calendar, use **Add Event → Add Existing Luma Event** with your university event's link. Do not create a duplicate listing.
- [ ] Wait for approval or requested changes in the university Slack channel. **Wait for approval before promoting or hosting.**
- [ ] Promote with the Luma event link; posters should include an event RSVP QR code. Follow the [marketing playbook](marketing.md).

## Run the session

- [ ] Arrive early with your co-Lead. Test the screen and decide who leads the slides and who supports students.
- [ ] Ask students to check in through the QR/link in the deck and claim ChatGPT Plus. Check-in opens **2 hours before** the Luma start time when the event is approved on the Global Luma calendar.
- [ ] Allow time to complete access steps. If access fails, help a student partner up and flag the issue to the program team.
- [ ] Introduce the activity, then let students do most of the work. Encourage small groups, comparing results, and peer support. Circulate and help.
- [ ] Take a few photos along the way, following the university's applicable requirements.
- [ ] Invite some students to share what they made.
- [ ] Ask everyone to complete the deck's check-out form before leaving. Students can check out up to **3 hours after** the Luma end time.
- [ ] Share the campus community channel and invite students to future Studio Sessions.

## Report and hand off

- [ ] In #collective-events, select the **Workshop Recap** workflow. Submission serves as the event report.
- [ ] Update the event brief and [active work](../operations/active-work.md) with actual outcomes, evidence, unresolved issues, and next steps. Do not store participant-level records here.

Use [the event brief template](../templates/event-brief.md) to record planning details. Any proposed minute-by-minute agenda is an adaptation, not a program requirement.
