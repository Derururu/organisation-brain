# Marketing guide

You’re welcome to promote your event in whatever ways you think would be most effective. We just ask that you make it clear the event is held by the OpenAI Student Collective, and don’t imply it is hosted by OpenAI directly.

When promoting your event more widely, give students a clear reason to show up. Say who the event is for and what they’ll get out of it, such as something they’ll make, learn, or try. Focus on what would appeal to that group of students, and keep your promises specific and realistic.

In the past, students have had success promoting their events by:

- Posting on their personal LinkedIn or Instagram.
- Personally inviting classmates to the first event.
- Asking a professor for a few minutes at the start of class.
- Sharing through a club newsletter or group chat.
- Putting up posters around campus.

You can also find inspiration in event recaps on Slack or ask other Leads what’s worked for them in the chat channel.

## Create materials with our ChatGPT Work skill

Our Marketing Materials skill for ChatGPT Work helps you create materials for your event using the Student Collective’s templates and brand guidelines. It starts with a gallery of workshop, studio session, and showcase designs. Choose one to adapt, or develop a new design for your campus.

1. Download the Marketing Materials skill ZIP from the Hub.
2. Upload it to a new chat in ChatGPT Work and ask it to get started.

## Create materials on your own

You’re welcome to create materials in your preferred design software. Whether you use our skill or design your own, please follow these guidelines.

- Follow the [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) when using our logo or name.
- Use the full name OpenAI Student Collective, and don’t imply that OpenAI directly hosts the event. This is an attribution requirement, not a requirement to print “student-led” on materials.
- Name the product your event uses: Codex or ChatGPT Work.
- Include the event date, time, and location in your finished materials.

Download the design resources to use as a starting point in your preferred design software.

The supplied artwork is in `assets/events/` and `assets/general/`. Additional design-resource downloads are available in the Hub.

## If you’re ever unsure

Ask your Community Coordinator about wording, a platform, or a design. We’re happy to help you think it through.
