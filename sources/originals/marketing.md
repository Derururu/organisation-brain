# Market your events

Transcribed from the user's message on 2026-10-20. Markdown formatting and pasted heading/HTML artefacts normalised. Publication/effective date not supplied.

You’re welcome to promote your event in whatever ways you think would be most effective. We just ask that you make it clear the event is held by the OpenAI Student Collective, and don’t imply it is hosted by OpenAI directly.

When promoting your event more widely, give students a clear reason to show up. Say who the event is for and highlight something specific students will make, learn, or try, and how it could be useful to them. Focus on what would appeal to that group of students, and keep your promises specific and realistic.

In the past, students have had success promoting their events by:

- Posting on their personal LinkedIn or Instagram.
- Personally inviting classmates to the first event.
- Asking a professor for a few minutes at the start of class.
- Sharing through a club newsletter or group chat.
- Putting up posters around campus.

You can also find inspiration in event recaps on Slack or ask other Leads what’s worked for them in the chat channel.

## Create materials on your own

You’re welcome to design from scratch or use our Canva templates below as a starting point. Whichever you choose, please follow these guidelines.

- Follow the [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) when using our logo or name.
  - Use the supplied Blossom as-is—don’t redraw, remix, stretch, rotate, or add effects.
  - Keep it black or white, with clear space around it and a simple background.
  - Keep the OpenAI Student Collective name clearly visible beside it, as in our templates. Don’t pair the Blossom with the OpenAI wordmark.
- Use the full name OpenAI Student Collective. Make clear that your event is student-led, rather than an official OpenAI-hosted event.
- Name the product your event uses: Codex or ChatGPT Work.
- Include the event date, time, and location in your finished materials.
