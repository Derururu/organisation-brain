# Studio Sessions

Transcribed from the user's message on 2026-10-20. Markdown formatting normalised. Original webpage URL and publication/effective date not supplied.

## Frequency

**8 over roughly half an academic year**

## Length

**60 minutes**

## Attendance

**10+ students**

Studio Sessions give students a regular time and place to work on projects, get support from peers, and work through questions that are holding them back. On some campuses, they might feel like office hours—a place to drop in, ask questions, and get unstuck. On others, they might feel like a build hour, with students working alongside one another on their own ideas.

Help students bring an innovative, impactful project to life on your campus—like:

- [Audio-first learning games for blind players](https://chatgpt.com/futures/#audemy)
- [A tool that helps street vendors manage their finances](https://chatgpt.com/futures/#crece-ai)
- [A student learning to become a pilot](https://chatgpt.com/futures/#ethan-truong)

At Studio Sessions you can help them take the first step and make progress on something they care about using ChatGPT or Codex, and leave with the confidence to keep going.

## Checklist

Use this checklist with your co-Lead to prepare for each session, support students while they work, and help them keep going afterwards.

### Getting ready

1. Choose a time and place students can return to—a library, lounge, or common area where they can work and talk. Add the event to your university’s Luma calendar.

   Note: You do not need to submit Studio Sessions to the central workshop calendar.

   [Create event](https://luma.com/create)

2. Aim for around ten students who have something they want to work on. Invite people from workshops, classes, and clubs, and encourage them to bring a friend. Share your Luma link so they know when and where to join.

   [Open the marketing guide](https://student-collective.openai.chatgpt.site/hosting/community/marketingmaterials)

3. Check the [Slack announcements](https://openai.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BRS7KLVS7) for updates to ChatGPT and Codex so you can share a highlight with students at the session.

### During the session

1. Share the check-in form with students when they arrive and ask them to check in.

   [Download QR code](https://student-collective.openai.chatgpt.site/images/hosting/studio-session-check-in-qr.png)

   Help students get started with the tools they need. As students check in, give them access to ChatGPT Plus if they don’t already have it.

   [Credits & Subscriptions](https://student-collective.openai.chatgpt.site/hosting/credits?from=studio-session)

2. Give students time to work, encourage them to ask questions, and connect students who can help each other. Invite students to share what they’re working on.

3. Help students keep building after the session. Before they leave, give them $20 Codex credits to continue their work.

   [Credits & Subscriptions](https://student-collective.openai.chatgpt.site/hosting/credits?from=studio-session)

### Afterwards

1. Open #collective-events on Slack and select the Studio Session Recap workflow. This serves as your event report.

   [Open #collective-events](https://openai.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BRS7QULGK)
